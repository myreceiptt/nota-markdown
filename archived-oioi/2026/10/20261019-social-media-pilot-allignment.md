## 1. What is already in place

The team has already established an Indonesia Instagram initiative, completed the reported Meta Business Suite integration, and created a detailed October–December content plan. This work should be preserved and used—not restarted.

| Source / asset | What we verified | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meeting minutes (14 Oct) | Parents prioritized; organic-first approach; roles and Meta setup discussed; strategy requested from Prof. | Direction exists, but some objectives and approval details need alignment. |
| Live content plan (Google Sheets) | 66 planned posts: Oct 22, Nov 21, Dec 23. October status: 6 Posted, 3 Approved, 13 blank. | A substantial production calendar already exists. |

## 2. The gap to close

The existing calendar is mainly educational, character-led, and engagement-oriented. Across the 66 entries, two explicitly feature Bommie Chat; none explicitly asks users to complete the free AI Profiling. That is appropriate for awareness, but it does not yet create a measurable path to product activation.

The meeting minutes emphasize initial awareness and organic follower growth, while other passages refer to installs, registration and profiling. These are best treated as sequential stages rather than competing objectives. Pris should confirm the phase priorities.

### Light-touch content quality fixes

- Keep the existing 66 entries as the working inventory. Do not create a parallel content calendar.
- Correct missing or stale statuses. The October mix summary says 9 single / 9 carousel / 4 reels, while the actual rows contain 10 single / 8 carousel / 4 reels.
- Check copy alignment before scheduling; for example, the 13 October emergency-preparedness carousel has a caption starter and CTA that appear to address a different topic.

---

## 3. Pilot objectives: staged, not competing

| Stage | Primary outcome | Minimum measurement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–30: Learn & stabilize | Consistent local awareness, useful engagement, sustainable workflow. | Reach, meaningful interactions, posts delivered, audience feedback. |
| Days 31–60: Acquire | Connect selected content to app discovery, store visits and registrations. | Profile/link clicks; app store traffic and registrations if attribution exists. |
| Days 61–90: Activate | Test whether social activity contributes to completed free AI Profiling and return visits. | Completed free AI Profiling and repeat usage, using aggregated app data. |

> **North-star learning question:** Which content and audience combinations move people from social media into a meaningful first product experience?
>
> No numeric growth promise should be made before reviewing a baseline and confirming tracking.

## 4. Adjust the existing content plan—not the workload

For the remaining October calendar and the November–December plan, retain the team’s creative themes and character assets. Reclassify posts by purpose and replace a limited number of existing slots with product-relevant content, subject to Pris’s approval.

| Content purpose | Example treatment | Suggested action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness / engagement | Characters, everyday situations, culture, self-discovery. | Keep the strongest; choose formats based on past response. |
| Parents & families | Child strengths, listening, family conversations, learning needs. | Prioritize within existing slots; avoid unsupported claims about children. |
| Product discovery | Show how the app works and what the free AI Profiling experience looks like. | Test one clear product-focused post per week by replacing, not adding, a slot. |
| Activation | Clear CTA to register and complete free AI Profiling. | Use only when link, app experience, and measurement are ready. |

### Publishing frequency must match capacity

The current plan schedules a post every weekday. Dhea has other CRM responsibilities, and the available time for this social media work is limited. Before locking that cadence, Pris and the team should compare estimated production hours with actual capacity. A lighter cadence (for example, 2–3 main posts per week plus repurposed stories) is an option to approve—not a unilateral change.

### Editorial guardrails

Use Janet’s brand guidelines, keep Indonesian audience language natural, check product demonstrations against the actual app, and avoid presenting AI-generated personality interpretations as medical or psychological diagnoses. Do not request sensitive children’s information through comments or DMs.

## 5. One live sheet, one weekly operating rhythm

The Google Sheet remains the source of truth. Add only three lightweight fields if the team finds them useful: **Primary Audience**, **Funnel Stage**, and **Content Owner**. Continue using the existing **Status** and **Link** fields; do not duplicate the plan in a new tracker.

**Recommended status vocabulary:**

`Draft → In Review → Approved → Scheduled → Posted → Measured`

Additional status: `On Hold`.

Existing historical statuses can be retained and normalized gradually.

| Weekly activity | Who leads | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before weekly review | Dhea / Sabrina | Updated statuses, performance snapshot, draft next-week slots and blockers. |
| 30-minute review | Pris, with team | Priorities, approved direction, decisions and unresolved issues. |
| After approval | Dhea / Sabrina | Content finalized and scheduled in Meta Business Suite. |
| During the week | Dhea / Sabrina | Publish/monitor/respond within available hours; escalate sensitive issues. |
| Periodic advisory | Prof. NOTA, as agreed | Strategic feedback and prioritized recommendations, not routine approvals. |

## 6. Responsibilities and decision rights

| Role | Accountability |
| --- | --- |
| Pris — Marketing Director | Final marketing priorities, editorial policy, campaign approval, cadence, and reporting expectations. |
| KK / Founders | Business sponsorship, resources, budget approval according to company authority. |
| Dhea — Indonesia coordinator (proposed) | Coordinate the living calendar, priorities, reporting and team workload within her assigned capacity. |

### Decision rule

The team can prepare and schedule routine content within an approved playbook. Exceptions, brand-sensitive claims, new channels, paid campaigns and material changes to the plan require Pris’s decision and any additional company approvals. The escalation path and turnaround expectations should be confirmed before the pilot begins.

## 8. Decisions requested from Pris and KK

- Confirm the 30–90 day sequence: awareness → acquisition → free AI Profiling activation.
- Approve the team’s weekly capacity, sustainable publishing cadence and delegated routine approval rules.
- Approve using the current Google Sheet as the only operational content plan, with minimal additions.
- Confirm access to aggregated app metrics through Yuku, plus the owner of any tracking setup.
- Decide whether CAIC boosting or other paid promotion should be scoped as a separate campaign with budget and measurement.

## 9. Reporting without extra bureaucracy

**Weekly:** One concise snapshot covering posts published, reach, meaningful engagement, profile/link clicks, and any available app activation data; followed by three lines: what worked, what needs adjustment, and what decision is needed.

**Monthly:** A short management view of trend, learnings and workload.

Avoid performance promises based solely on follower counts.

## 10. Advisory boundary

Prof. NOTA provides recommendations and scheduled strategic reviews. The Indonesia team operates the channels, Pris owns marketing decisions, and founders approve resources. Any expanded recurring management responsibility, extra deliverables or response-time commitment should be separately agreed with KK under an updated advisory scope. This staffing/contract discussion is outside the team’s operating SOP.
