## 2. Pembuka

Hi everyone, nice to meet you all.

KK and I have discussed a little about how I can help the social media team.

But before we start talking about strategies, content, or advertising, I'd like to understand what the team is already doing and what we actually want to achieve.

From what I understand, one of our main goals is to bring more people to the iBLOOMING mobile app.

But I think we should also look beyond downloads. We need to understand whether people actually register, use the app, explore our products, and come back again.

So for today, I'd prefer to have an open discussion.

I'd like to learn about the team, the accounts we're managing, what has been working, what hasn't, and what resources we have.

After that, I can prepare a clearer strategy and we can discuss how to execute it together.

I don't want to change what the team is doing before I understand it properly.

Tidak semua pertanyaan wajib selesai hari ini. Yang paling penting adalah memahami objectives, existing assets, ownership, dan measurement readiness.

## 4. Jangan menetapkan KPI angka sebelum ada baseline

Aku ingin kamu membedakan empat tahapan berikut ketika KK atau tim berbicara tentang pertumbuhan pengguna.

Awareness

Reach · Views · Engagement

Acquisition

App Store Visits · Installs

Activation

Registration · First Meaningful Action

Retention & Product Value

Returning Users · Product Usage · Purchases

Kerangka awal user journey, bukan angka konversi aktual iBLOOMING.

Kalau objective KK adalah more downloads, itu sah sebagai target awal. Namun kamu perlu menyarankan agar tim juga mengukur berapa banyak pengguna yang benar-benar melakukan aktivitas bermakna setelah instalasi.

Kita tidak ingin mengulang masalah yang kamu ceritakan tentang rendahnya penggunaan aplikasi meskipun basis penggunanya besar. Insight itu relevan, tetapi tetap perlu diverifikasi sebelum dipakai sebagai fakta dalam pitch deck Alfamart.
