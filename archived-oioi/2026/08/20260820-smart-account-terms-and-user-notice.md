# 1. Smart Account in Plain Language

## What is a Smart Account?

A **Smart Account** is an on-chain account created for you after you log in to your BGC × iBLOOMING account and complete the **Activate Smart Account** steps.

Think of it as your personal Web3 account inside the BGC × iBLOOMING ecosystem.

It may later be used for supported features such as:

* digital receipts;
* product ownership records;
* product authenticity or product passports;
* warranty records;
* access to digital products or content;
* course or event access;
* digital credentials;
* supported rewards or benefits;
* supported on-chain payments or transactions;
* other BGC × iBLOOMING Web3-enabled products introduced in the future.

Your Smart Account is designed to make these Web3 functions easier to use without requiring you to understand the technical complexities of blockchain and Web3.

---

# 2. A Smart Account Is Not an Investment Product

Your Smart Account itself is **not**:

* a bank account;
* a savings account;
* a deposit product;
* an investment product;
* a token;
* a cryptocurrency;
* a promise of financial return;
* a promise of rewards;
* a promise that any digital asset will increase in value.

Creating or using a Smart Account does not automatically give you any right to:

* Purchase Credit;
* Sales Points;
* BGC rewards;
* iBLOOMING rewards;

Any product, credential, entitlement, reward, token, or other benefit must be governed by its own applicable rules.

# 3. When Your Smart Account Is Activated

Your Smart Account becomes available after you successfully log in to your BGC × iBLOOMING account and complete the **Activate Smart Account** process.

If you have not yet activated your Smart Account, BGC × iBLOOMING may invite you to do so.

The activation process generally works as follows:

1. You log in to your BGC × iBLOOMING account.
2. You select **Activate Smart Account**.
3. You are shown these Smart Account Terms and any applicable Privacy Notice.
4. You review and accept the applicable terms.
5. BGC × iBLOOMING creates an embedded or in-app Personal Wallet (Web3 wallet: EOA) associated with your BGC × iBLOOMING account identity.
6. A Smart Account is then provisioned for that identity and controlled through the associated Personal Wallet.
7. After activation is complete, you may optionally connect your existing compatible external Web3 wallets for supported wallet-management and transaction features.

You do not need an existing compatible external Web3 wallet to activate your Smart Account.

An external Web3 wallet cannot be used in place of your Personal Wallet associated with your BGC × iBLOOMING account as the primary controller of your Smart Account under the current architecture.

---

# 4. Your Personal Wallet and Smart Account

When you activate your Smart Account, BGC × iBLOOMING creates or provisions two related blockchain components for your identity:

## Personal Wallet

Your Personal Wallet is an embedded or in-app wallet associated with your BGC × iBLOOMING identity.

It acts as the primary wallet used to control your Smart Account.

## Smart Account

Your Smart Account is a programmable blockchain account associated with your Personal Wallet.

It may be used for supported BGC × iBLOOMING features such as digital receipts, product ownership, access credentials, product passports, supported digital assets, and other blockchain-enabled services.

The intended relationship is:

```text
BGC × iBLOOMING Account
        ↓
Personal Wallet (Web3 wallet: EOA) as BGC × iBLOOMING Identity
        ↓
Smart Account
```

Under the current architecture, the Personal Wallet and Smart Account are provisioned as part of the Smart Account activation process.

# 5. Connecting an External Web3 Wallet

After your Smart Account has been activated, you may optionally connect one or more compatible External Web3 Wallets that you already control.

Examples may include compatible wallets provided by third-party wallet applications.

Connecting an External Web3 Wallet does not replace:

* your BGC × iBLOOMING Personal Wallet;
* your Smart Account;
* or the relationship between your Personal Wallet and Smart Account.

Your External Web3 Wallet remains separately controlled by you.

BGC × iBLOOMING may provide an interface that allows you to view, select, or interact with a connected External Web3 Wallet from within supported BGC × iBLOOMING applications.

For example, you may be able to switch the active wallet used for supported interactions between:

* your BGC × iBLOOMING Smart Account; and
* a connected External Web3 Wallet.

This may allow you to perform supported actions, including transferring supported assets from your External Web3 Wallet to your Smart Account, without leaving the BGC × iBLOOMING application.

Any transaction initiated from an External Web3 Wallet still requires the authorization required by that wallet and may also be subject to applicable blockchain network fees.

Connecting an External Web3 Wallet does not give BGC × iBLOOMING ownership of, custody over, or unrestricted control over that External Web3 Wallet or the assets held in it.

# 6. Who Controls Your Smart Account?

Under the current architecture, your Smart Account is primarily controlled through the Personal Wallet associated with your BGC × iBLOOMING identity.

Your Personal Wallet is created as an embedded or in-app Web3 wallet during the **Activate Smart Account** process.

BGC × iBLOOMING intends to use wallet infrastructure that protects the underlying wallet key material without storing a plaintext private key on BGC × iBLOOMING application servers.

Your connected External Web3 Wallets do not become controllers of your Smart Account merely because they are connected to the BGC × iBLOOMING application.

BGC × iBLOOMING does not become entitled to your digital assets merely because it provides, provisions, displays, or supports your Personal Wallet, Smart Account, or connected External Web3 Wallets.

Smart Accounts are programmable smart contracts. Their technical permissions, recovery capabilities, upgradeability, and administrative controls depend on the Smart Account implementation used in production.

Before production launch, BGC × iBLOOMING will determine and disclose, where applicable:

* whether the Smart Account contract is upgradeable;
* who can authorize an upgrade;
* what recovery permissions exist;
* what administrative permissions exist;
* what role the Personal Wallet has in authorizing Smart Account actions;
* and what notice or user approval is required for material changes.

# 7. Your Smart Account Address

Your Smart Account will have its own blockchain address.

That address is different from the address of your Personal Wallet, even though the Personal Wallet is used to control the Smart Account.

The Smart Account address may be deterministically assigned or calculated from your identity and wallet configuration.

In some implementations, the Smart Account address may be calculated and displayed before the Smart Account contract is actually deployed on-chain.

Actual on-chain deployment may occur:

* during Smart Account activation; or
* when you perform your first supported on-chain action.

The exact deployment process may depend on the supported blockchain network and the production infrastructure configuration.

* the wallet being used;
* the receiving address;
* the asset;
* the amount;
* the blockchain network;
* the transaction fee;
* the action being authorized.

BGC × iBLOOMING may provide user-interface safeguards, transaction previews, confirmation steps, warnings, or other protections.

Transactions initiated using a connected External Web3 Wallet remain subject to the authorization mechanisms and security model of that External Web3 Wallet.

# 11. Network Fees and Gas Sponsorship

Blockchain transactions may require network transaction fees, commonly called **gas fees**.

For selected actions, BGC × iBLOOMING may sponsor some or all applicable network fees through a paymaster or similar blockchain infrastructure.

Gas sponsorship:

* may apply only to selected transactions;
* may be subject to limits;
* may depend on network conditions;
* may depend on the wallet or transaction type;
* may be changed or discontinued;
* is not a permanent entitlement unless expressly stated otherwise.

If a transaction is not sponsored, you may be required to pay the applicable network fee.

Where reasonably practicable, the application should display relevant transaction-fee information before you authorize the transaction.

BGC × iBLOOMING does not control the underlying blockchain network or the fees charged by that network.

# 13. Account Recovery and Personal Wallet Access

Recovery of your BGC × iBLOOMING Account and recovery of blockchain-wallet access are related but may involve different technical processes.

For the Personal Wallet created during Smart Account activation, supported recovery mechanisms may be provided through the wallet infrastructure and the BGC × iBLOOMING account-recovery process.

The exact recovery procedure, identity-verification requirements, and available recovery methods will depend on the production wallet implementation.

BGC × iBLOOMING will disclose the applicable recovery process before production use where required.

Recovery of your BGC × iBLOOMING Account does not automatically mean that BGC × iBLOOMING can recover every blockchain wallet or digital asset associated with you.

For a connected External Web3 Wallet, BGC × iBLOOMING generally cannot recover:

* its private key;
* its seed phrase;
* access credentials controlled by the external wallet provider;
* or assets lost because access to that External Web3 Wallet has been permanently lost.

You remain responsible for following the recovery and security procedures applicable to your External Web3 Wallet.
