## 4. Final Findings

### 4.1 BGC and iBLOOMING Have Different Economic Engines

- BGC is primarily affiliate-entry and LTS driven.
- iBLOOMING is primarily product-transaction driven.
- Recorded iBLOOMING product cash-in is very small compared with BGC affiliate cash-in.

### 4.2 Existing-System Tokenization Is Not Recommended

PC, SP, LTS, rewards, pools, and payouts must not be converted into a token under the archived Whitepaper and Tokenflow assumptions.

### 4.3 SIMALPHA Is Useful but Preliminary

SIMALPHA is a real internal decision-support MVP.

Its first reports do not prove:

- sustainability;
- profit;
- reserve adequacy;
- member-level fairness;
- tax optimization;
- token readiness.

### 4.4 Web3 Login Remains Valid

Web3 Login has independent value for:

- identity;
- wallet readiness;
- ownership;
- access;
- credentials;
- receipts;
- Product-Led pilots.

### 4.5 Product Demand Is the Strategic Priority

The next program should test and increase demand for real physical and digital products independently of affiliate-income promises.
